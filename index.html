Hot Spot Automatic Analysis & Tracking

Fully automatic. No manual number entry is required. The system analyzes the 12-hour collection window, selects the two 5-number groups whose co-occurrence is least likely to be pure chance (statistically corrected for the number of groups tested, not just raw frequency), evaluates timing stability, estimates the next appearance window, and tracks performance in 20-draw blocks.

Note: Hot Spot draws are independent random events — each draw does not "remember" past draws. Past frequency, however rigorously measured, does not improve the odds of future draws. This tool is for statistical tracking and entertainment only, not a prediction system.
